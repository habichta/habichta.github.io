Hi, I'm Arthur Habicht, co-founder and CTO of [Helpany](https://helpany.com) and an engineer working in machine learning and AI.

I build machine learning systems that need to work outside the demo. At Helpany, that means turning raw sensor data into insights that senior living communities rely on every day. What I enjoy most is the moment a piece of technology stops being a prototype and becomes a product people trust.

My work spans the full stack of getting there: machine learning algorithms, real-time sensor and batched data pipelines, software development, cloud and on-prem infrastructure, hardware product development and certification, information security, as well as engineering management and product and business strategy. Our radar device, Paul, was named one of [TIME's Best Inventions of 2026](https://time.com/collection/best-inventions/2026/helpany-paul/).

Before Helpany I worked as a machine learning and software engineer at ti&m in Zurich, where I built the internal AI/ML team and delivered proof-of-concepts in document classification and question answering for clients in the financial industry. I also teach unsupervised learning and software architecture for data science as a visiting lecturer at the University of Lucerne.

I hold a B.Sc and M.Sc in Computer Science from ETH Zurich, with a focus on distributed systems and machine learning. I wrote my master thesis in Prof. Andreas Krause's [Learning & Adaptive Systems group](https://las.inf.ethz.ch/) at ETH, on deep reinforcement learning with real-world data for the control of PV power plants.

You can reach me by [email](mailto:habichta.shore104@passmail.net), or find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/habichta/) and [GitHub](https://github.com/habichta).
